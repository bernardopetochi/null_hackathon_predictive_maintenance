Method

How we know it works.

Real failures are rare, so we train on 1,000 simulated freezers built from data on 53 real ones. Then we check it on real biobank freezers, and on a real hydraulic test rig.

Test results aboveSimulated freezers only

On real freezers (Danish National Biobank, model unchanged): leak in freezer 806278 flagged 20.8 days before the service date, about 18 days before a −70 °C alarm, but that freezer was used to calibrate the model. A second leak (806274) was missed: the alert came 2 hours after the −70 °C alarm. A healthy freezer (806019) raised false alarms in 16 of 32 four-month periods. The simulated false-alarm rate does not carry over yet; re-tuning the alarm level on real healthy freezers did not fix it, so the next step is retraining the model with real healthy data and the real leak pattern. Details in analysis/REAL_BACKTEST.md.
